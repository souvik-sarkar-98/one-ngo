What the Builder is

OneNGO has no fixed screens for "donations" or "volunteers". Everything people see is described by settings called definitions, and the Builder is where you create and change them. For example:

  • a record type says what information you keep, such as a donation with an amount and a donor;
  • a workflow says the stages it goes through, such as Raised, then Paid;
  • a list says how people browse those records;
  • a message template says what email goes out.

You build these up like blocks. When you are happy, you publish, and everyone sees the change straight away. Nothing you type affects anyone until you publish it.

New to this?Follow the step-by-step set-up of a small module. Saving and publishingHow drafts, Check and Publish work on every screen. Something refused?Common messages and what to do about them.
Safe by designEvery change is kept as a version. You can see what changed, go back to an earlier version, and nothing you publish rewrites old records. Some rules can never be broken by any setting: accounts always balance, closed years stay closed, a receipt number is never reused, and one organisation never sees another's data.

Words used in this guide

Definition
One saved piece of setup: a record type, a workflow, a template and so on.
Kind
The sort of definition, for example "Record type" or "Automation".
Record type
A kind of thing you keep a register of: donors, donations, beneficiaries, assets. Think of it as a form plus the register it fills.
Record
One entry in that register, such as one donation.
Field
One box on the form, such as "Amount" or "Date of birth".
Label
The name people see. You can change it whenever you like.
Key
The short internal name the system uses, made from the label (for example "Volunteer shift" becomes volunteerShift). Once published it is fixed, so the label can change without breaking anything.
Draft
Your unpublished changes. Only people in the Builder see them.
Publish
Make the draft live for everyone. It creates a new numbered version (v1, v2…).
Module
A ready-made bundle of definitions shipped with the product, such as "Donations". Your changes to it are kept separately (as a "patch") so product updates still reach you.
Workflow, status, step
A workflow is the path a record follows. Each stop on the path is a status (also called a state), such as "Raised". A step is a button that moves the record from one status to another, such as "Mark as paid".
Role and permission
A permission is one thing a person may do, such as "Edit donations". A role is a named bundle of permissions, such as "Finance". People are given roles.
Formula (expression)
A short calculation or condition, such as record.amount > 0. Used for "only when" conditions and calculated fields. See Formula boxes.
Placeholder
A gap in a message filled from the record, written in double curly brackets, such as {{ record.number }}.
Chip
A quick-filter button above a list, such as "Mine" or "Outstanding".
Connector
A saved link to an outside service: SMS, WhatsApp, a webhook, or another website's API.

Your first set-up, step by step

This example builds a small "Volunteer shift" register: people sign up for shifts, a coordinator approves them, and the volunteer gets an email. Every Builder module is made the same way.

  1. Create the record type. In the Builder, open Record types and press New record type. Type the Name "Volunteer shift" and press Create.
  2. Add the fields. Press Add field for each box you need: Volunteer (Person), Shift date (Date), Hours (Number), Notes (Long text). Tick Required where a value must be given. Choose a Title field so each record has a readable name.
  3. Publish the record type. Press Check, fix anything it lists, then Publish… and Publish. Other definitions can only point to a record type once it is published.
  4. Add a workflow. Open Workflows, press New workflow, and choose the record type "Volunteer shift". Add the statuses Requested, Approved and Done, and steps such as "Approve" (from Requested to Approved). Under Approval, tick Needs approval if a coordinator must agree. Publish.
  5. Add a list. Open Lists, press New list, and pick the record type. Add chips (for example "Mine" and "Requested"), choose the table columns, and build the create form. Watch the preview on the right. Publish.
  6. Write the message. Open Message templates, press New message template, and write a Subject and Message using placeholders such as {{ record.shiftDate }}. Use Send a test to me. Publish.
  7. Send it automatically. Open Automations, press New automation, and set the Event to "The status changes" and To status to "approved". Add an action "Notify people", pick the template, and send to the volunteer. Publish.
  8. Give people access. In Access (or Administration › Roles), give the volunteer role "See" and "Create" on Volunteer shifts, and the coordinator role the "Approve" step.
  9. Put it in the menu. Open Layout, use Add a record to add Volunteer shifts to the navigation bar, then Publish layout.
TipPublish things in the order they depend on each other: the record type first, then the workflow, list, templates and automations that use it.

The editor screen

Every definition opens in the same frame. Only the middle part (the Visual tab) differs by kind; it is described in the sections that follow.

How saving works

  • There is no Save button. Your changes save themselves about a second after you stop typing, when you leave a box, and when you leave the page.
  • Changes go into a draft. People using the app see nothing until you publish.
  • For definitions that came with a module, the draft is kept in this browser only ("Draft on this device"). Publish before you switch computers, or your edits will not be there.

Formula boxes

Many settings take a short formula, for example "Only when", "Visible when", "Calculated as" or "Must be true". A formula is like a spreadsheet formula. It reads the record's fields and gives back a value, often true or false.

Examples

record.amount > 0                      the amount is more than zero
record.amount >= 50000                 fifty thousand rupees or more
record.status == 'raised'              the record is in the Raised status
isEmpty(record.phone)                  no phone number was given
record.quantity * record.rate          a calculated total
daysBetween(record.startDate, today()) days since the start date

Text goes in quotes. Use and, or, not; compare with ==, !=, <, <=, >, >=; and do arithmetic with + - * / %. a ? b : c means "if a then b, otherwise c".

Common messages and fixes

You seeWhat it means and what to do
"You can look at this definition; changing it needs permission to publish."Your role can only look. Ask an administrator for "Change and publish the setup".
"Editing works best on a tablet or computer…"You are on a phone. Use a larger screen to edit.
"Fix these problems before publishing:"Read each line, fix it on the Visual tab, then press Check again.
"record type '…' is not published"Publish the record type first, then the definition that uses it.
"unknown field '…'" / "unknown status '…'"A field or status was renamed or removed. Pick it again from the list.
"… is still used by …. Change those first."Open the definitions listed under Used by and stop using the field there, then remove it.
"⚠ Required but not on the form: …"Add those fields to the list's create form, or nobody can create a record.
"⚠ No role holds this, so nobody sees …"On the Access map, give that permission to a role, or change what the element requires.
"'…' is not a generated permission and no role has it yet"A warning: you typed a new permission. Give it to a role, or fix a typing mistake.
"Not balanced: debit …, credit …"The posting rule's debit and credit lines do not add up to the same amount. Check the Amount formulas.
"Only one primary workflow may have no condition"Give all but one primary workflow a "Chosen when" condition.
"Draft kept on this device"Edits to a module definition are only in this browser. Publish them before switching computers.
A change does not appear for usersCheck it was published (the badge says "Published v…" with no "Draft"), that their role has the permission, and ask them to reload the screen.
