Financial year

Domain: finance · Entity: financial year

Implementation-independent · reconciled against backend + frontend

1. Purpose

Each organisation keeps its books, receipts and record numbers by financial year. The organisation chooses the month its financial year starts in (Indian NGOs usually start on 1 April, which is the default). Everything that is counted or closed "per year" follows that choice: which year a payment is booked into, when receipt numbers start again from 1, when record numbers such as donation numbers start again, and how totals are grouped by year on dashboards. Once the organisation has started using its years, the start month is fixed so that closed years and issued receipt numbers never change.

2. Actors

  • Organisation administrator (with the organisation-settings permission) — sees the start month and may change it while the organisation has not yet opened a financial year or issued a receipt. Every change is recorded in the audit log with the old and new month.
  • Finance staff — record payments, which are booked into the open financial year containing the payment date, and receive numbered receipts that restart each year.
  • Platform operator / setup — opens, closes and reopens financial years for an organisation. Opening the first year fixes the start month. When an organisation is onboarded, the operator's request sets its start month and opens the requested year, in that order.
  • Dashboard viewers — see totals grouped by the organisation's own financial year.

3. Domain model

ConceptBusiness meaning
Financial year start monthThe calendar month (January to December) on whose first day each financial year of the organisation begins. April unless the organisation chose otherwise.
Financial yearTwelve consecutive months from the first day of the start month to the last day of the month before it. Each organisation has its own years.
Year labelHow a year is named in the books, receipt numbers and record numbers. A year that starts in January is named by its calendar year (2026). A year that starts in any other month spans two calendar years and is named by the year it starts in and the last two digits of the next year (2026-27 for April 2026 to March 2027, or for July 2026 to June 2027).
Financial periodA financial year opened in the books. It is open (entries may be booked into it) or closed (nothing can be booked until it is reopened with a reason).
Receipt seriesA numbered sequence of receipts. A series that restarts per year numbers receipts within each financial year, with the year label in the number (RCPT/2026-27/00001).
Record numberingA record type's numbering may restart per financial year and may show the year label (DON/2026-27/00001).

4. Business rules

  • The financial year start month is a per-organisation setting from January to December; it is April unless changed. Two organisations on the platform may use different start months, and each one's years are independent of the other's.
  • A payment or other ledger entry is booked into the financial year that contains its date, by the organisation's start month. If that year has not been opened the entry is refused ("Financial year 2027-28 is not open"); if it is closed the entry is refused ("Financial year 2026 is closed").
  • A financial year can only be opened under the label that matches the organisation's start month: an organisation starting in January opens 2026, never 2026-27, and the other way round. The year's first and last days follow from the label and the start month.
  • Receipts in a series that restarts per year are numbered from 1 again on the first day of each of the organisation's financial years; the number carries that year's label. Numbers are gap-free and never reused.
  • Record numbers that restart per financial year use the organisation's current financial year on the day the record is created.
  • Totals grouped "by financial year" on pages and widgets group by the organisation's own years and are labelled "FY <year label>".
  • History is protected: the start month cannot change once the organisation has opened any financial year or issued any receipt. The change is refused with the message that closed years and receipt numbers must stay as they are. Saving the same month again is not a change and is accepted.
  • Opening a year or issuing a receipt and changing the start month cannot overlap: whichever happens first wins, so a change can never slip in between the check and the first year being opened.

5. User flows

Find / view

The organisation settings screen shows "Financial year starts in" with the month name in the person's language. Once the start month is fixed it is shown read-only with an explanation of why.

Create

Every organisation starts with April. Nothing needs to be created.

Update

Before the books are started, an administrator chooses another month and saves the settings. The change is audited. After a year has been opened or a receipt issued, the screen no longer offers the choice and the organisation refuses any attempt to change it.

Other actions

Opening, closing and reopening years, booking entries, issuing receipts and numbering records all follow the start month as described in the business rules.

7. Out of scope / non-goals

  • Moving an organisation to a different start month after it has started its books (re-dating closed years, renumbering receipts or records). The rule is to refuse; any such move would be a separate, audited migration.
  • Short or long transitional years (for example a nine-month year when changing the start month).
  • Financial years that start on a day other than the first of a month, or follow a 52/53-week calendar.
  • A screen to open, close or reopen financial years; today this is done during setup.
