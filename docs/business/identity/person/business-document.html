Person

Domain: identity · Entity: person

Implementation-independent · reconciled against backend + frontend

1. Purpose

Keep one membership for each person in the organisation, with a profile the organisation can extend, a read-only directory for colleagues, and administration for invitations, roles and the full profile.

2. Actors

  • Any signed-in person opens My profile from their name on More. They see and change only the profile fields the organisation marked as editable by the person.
  • Someone who can see people opens the People destination and looks up active colleagues. The list is read-only.
  • Someone who can manage people uses More → Administration → People to add, invite, suspend, reactivate and edit any profile field, and assigns roles.
  • Someone who can manage roles uses Administration → Roles to add people to a role or remove them.
  • A configurer adds profile fields in the Builder and marks each as editable by the person and/or shown in the directory.

3. Domain model

ConceptBusiness meaning
PersonA member of the organisation: name, email, roles and status (invited, active or suspended). People are never deleted; on request they can be erased, which pseudonymises them (see Consent and erasure).
ProfileThe extra details of that person. One profile exists for every member, created with them, and it can be empty. The organisation chooses the fields.
DirectoryThe read-only list of active people, plus the profile fields marked for colleagues to see.

4. Business rules

  • Name and email belong to the account. A person does not change them on My profile.
  • The directory shows active people only. Invited and suspended people stay on the administration list.
  • A suspended person's profile cannot be changed until they are reactivated. Their name stays on records and in the audit log.
  • My profile opens read-only. Edit is offered only when at least one field is editable by that person. Save sends only the fields they changed.
  • Fields an administrator sets are not shown on My profile. Administrators still see and change the whole profile.
  • A sensitive field shown in the directory is masked unless the viewer may see sensitive profile details.
  • Sensitive values (for example the phone number and emergency contact on a profile, or a donor's PAN) are stored encrypted with the organisation's current key. People who may see them, and the organisation's own automations and messages, read them as entered; everyone else still sees them masked. They are never kept readable in search or in the activity history.
  • A sensitive value marked unique is still unique: a second person or record with the same value (ignoring capitals) is refused, and imports still find a record by it, without the value being decrypted for the check.
  • When the key changes, values stored under the old key stay readable until a background job stores them again under the new key. The same job encrypts values saved before encryption was introduced.
  • The profile does not appear among Records.
  • A change to someone's roles, a role's permissions, or their membership status (for example suspension) takes effect on their next action, and within 5 seconds wherever else they are signed in.
  • Proposed, awaiting product-owner approval: someone with the erase permission can erase a suspended person. Their profile's personal fields are cleared (kind, dates and other non-personal choices stay), and, when they belong to no other organisation, their account's name and email become a pseudonym and the account is switched off. An account shared with another organisation is left unchanged. Records that name the person keep pointing at them and show the pseudonym. Consent the person gave is withdrawn. The full rules are in the Consent and erasure document.

5. User flows

Find / view

People who can see people open the People destination. They search, filter and open a person read-only. There is no invite, edit or role change on that screen.

Create

An administrator adds a person from Administration → People, with email, name, roles, optional profile values and an optional invitation. The profile is created even when those values are left blank.

Update

From More, the account card (name, email and organisation, with no extra label) opens My profile. The person reads their editable fields, chooses Edit, changes them and saves. Cancel returns to the read-only view. An administrator edits every profile field from the person's panel in Administration.

Other actions

Administration → People resends an invitation, suspends and reactivates, and sets the person's roles. Administration → Roles adds or removes people on a role. The Builder remains the place that adds profile fields and the two switches "Editable by the person themselves" and "Shown in the directory".

6. Fields & validation

FieldRulesVisibility / conditions
Name, emailRequired to add a person. Email identifies the account.Shown on the directory and on My profile. Not edited there.
Roles, statusStatus is invited, active or suspended.Roles appear in the directory. Status appears only in Administration.
Profile fieldsDefined by the organisation. Each can be editable by the person, shown in the directory, or both. Conditions can hide a field.Directory shows only fields marked for it. My profile shows only fields the person may change. Administration shows all of them.

7. Out of scope / non-goals

  • Deleting a person (erasure pseudonymises instead).
  • Listing profiles under Records.
  • Letting a person change another person's profile, or a field an administrator owns.

9. Discrepancies (backend vs frontend)

TopicBackend behaviorFrontend behaviorResolution
Fields on My profile The own-profile read returns every profile field, and marks which ones the person may change. A save of any other field is refused. My profile shows only the fields the person may change. The save rule is authoritative. Hiding the other fields is presentation.
A profile field that points at another person The person may change it when it is marked editable by them. My profile does not offer that kind of field. An administrator can still set it. A person-picker on My profile is not provided.
