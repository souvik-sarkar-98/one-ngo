Step-by-step navigation — one set of controls for phone and desktop

Every journey, step by step, with the phone and the desktop side by side. Both use exactly the same controls, drawn from the same components. Only the arrangement adapts to the width: list rows (cards on phones, a table on wide screens), where the navigation sits (bottom bar or side rail, with the same four items), and where panels appear (sliding up from the bottom on phones, centred on wide screens). The yellow outline marks what the person taps next.

Same navigationApp bar with the product name, organisation and account. The page title sits in the content, with Back on that same row. Default destinations: Home, Records, Inbox, More. A published navigation can put record lists and pages on that same bar and rail. Bottom bar on phones, side rail on desktop. More stays last.
Same list controlsStatus chips, search box, Sort and Filters buttons, removable filter pills, row menu (⋮), the create button (+), and infinite scroll with "Loading more…".
Same panelsFilters, record detail, edit, workflow steps, create stepper, and reject reason all use one panel component with the same fields and buttons.
Same addressEvery step has an address. Back, reload and shared links reproduce the exact screen on any device.
Signed-off detailsNo record counts on Home or Records (names and descriptions only). The record panel is a single column on every size; on wide screens it is only centred. Tablet tables use the list view's optional compactColumns. Existing donations are not linked to donors automatically. Record groups get a visual editor in the Builder. Full specification: .cursor/docs/design/ngo-app-ui-design.md.
What this changes from the earlier decisions.
  • The desktop table layout no longer has its own toolbar, tabs, filter card and record page. It uses the same chips, search, filter panel, detail panel and forms as phones. Only the rows differ: table rows instead of cards.
  • The separate record page goes away. A record always opens in the record panel: a bottom sheet on phones, a centred panel on desktop.
  • The desktop side navigation becomes a rail with the same four items as the phone's bottom bar. Record types are listed on the Records screen instead of in the side navigation.
  • The Inbox is split into two chips, Approvals and Notifications (/inbox?tab=…). Approvals use the same cards on both, and the desktop approvals table goes away. The Inbox badge counts only approvals waiting for you.
  • Donors become a record type. A donation picks a donor, and the donor's details are copied onto the donation; a donor's record lists their donations.
  • Home and the Records screen group record types (for example Fundraising: Donors, Donations, Pledges). Groups come from a new "Record groups" definition, which defaults to grouping by module.
  • The Builder editors stay desktop-only; phones show the catalogue read-only.
First-release additions — journeys 10 to 20 (approved 2026-10-24)Home with widgets and pins, pages in More, language choice, record actions, the receipt print view, CSV import and export, files, sharing, selection and bulk actions, location / signature / rich-text fields, and a record with two workflows (Design §13a). They use only the controls above; three additions appear: the list's own ⋮ at the end of the toolbar (Pin to Home, Select, Import, Export), a ⋮ in the record panel header (Share), and the bulk bar while rows are selected. Examples come from the Donations module and from a Beneficiary record (BEN/2026-27/00012) for files, location, signature and two workflows.
Breadcrumb on every route (added and approved 2026-10-24)One line under the app bar on every screen, derived from the address and the definitions, never drawn by a screen itself: the destination first, with no leading "Home" (decided 2026-10-24), then for records the group, the record type, the open record (number · title) or "New …", and the panel mode where it means something (Edit, a step, an action, Share, Import). For example Records › Fundraising › Donations › DON/2026-27/00040 · Ravi Kumar › Mark as paid; Inbox › Approvals; More › Administration › Roles › Finance. Every item but the last links back (lists keep their own filters and sort); the last is the current page. Phones show the first and last items with "…" in between (tap it for the full trail; Journey 3.2), and long last items end with "…". No breadcrumb on sign-in or the print view.
People, profiles and Administration — journeys 21 to 23 (added and approved 2026-10-24)Administration (/admin, from More, administrators only) holds People, role assignment, connectors, API keys, background jobs, the audit log and organisation settings with storage (Journey 23); the Builder keeps only definitions. People are not a destination in the bar or in More, and /people opens Home. Administrators open Administration at /admin from More: People (/admin/people: status chips, the create button, row ⋮, adding, invitations, role changes, suspending) and Roles (/admin/roles: who has each role). What each role allows is defined in the Builder, not here. Everyone opens My profile by tapping their name at the top of More, and changes only the fields marked "The person can change this"; the rest show "Set by your administrator".
Shell chrome (updated 2026-10-25)The blue app bar does not change with the page. It shows the product name NGOOne, the organisation logo when the organisation has one (same-origin or embedded image only), and the organisation name, plus the inbox bell and the account initials. The page name is a heading in the content, left-aligned. Back sits on that same row, immediately left of the heading, and only when the screen is not Home, Records, Inbox or More. Clicking or hovering the initials opens Sign out. Choosing it asks for confirmation: this device will be signed out and the person must sign in again. More has no Sign out button, and it does not repeat the record groups; those stay on Home and Records.
HTTP calls and account sign-in (added 2026-10-31)Connectors gain two kinds, drawn in the journey after Administration. HTTP call is either Send after save (queued, retried, does not undo the save) or Look up while saving (fills record fields before the save; a failure stays under the input and nothing is saved). Account sign-in stores one organisation's access to a provider; an HTTP call can sign in with it. One HTTP connector can be marked to send all of the organisation's email, then the organisation's mail server, then the installation mail server. The Builder adds a Call a connector step (send connectors only) and a Lookups tab on a record type. Filled fields are read only on the record. Phones review Builder screens; editing stays on larger screens, as with record groups.
Theme, layout and organisation information (wireframe 2026-11-01)An administrator with Manage organisation settings changes these from Administration, without opening the Builder. Drawn step by step in org-admin-appearance.html: organisation information (name, legal name, contact, address, registrations, logo), the theme (the same colour tokens, with a live preview and a contrast check), and the layout (the same published navigation, plus the starting list layout on wide screens). Theme and layout stay the published definitions; either this screen or the Builder edits them.
Custom navigation (added 2026-11-01)One published navigation, arranged in the Builder, chooses what sits on the bar and the rail and what sits in More. A slot can be Home, Records, Inbox, People, a record list, a page, Administration, Builder or Settings. More has no profile. My profile, Language and About this app are a separate screen, Settings, opened from a Settings tile in More. Without a published navigation, the four default destinations and the More page in the earlier journeys stay as drawn. The phone bar keeps the first four slots, then More. Further sidebar items stay on the desktop rail and are listed at the top of the phone's More sheet. Tapping More does not leave the current screen: phones open the existing bottom sheet; wide screens open a panel beside the rail, not the centred dialog. Items the person cannot read are left out and the bar closes up. The rail highlights the record list itself when that list is on the sidebar; the breadcrumb still follows the address.
