Organisation administrator — theme, layout and organisation information

Asha Shetty is an administrator of Demo Foundation. From Administration she changes how the app looks and who the organisation is. She does not open the Builder. Phone and desktop use the same fields and buttons. Width only changes the arrangement: a single column on a phone, the form beside a live preview on a wide screen. The yellow outline marks what she taps next.

Who can change itManage organisation settings (admin:settings). People without it do not see Theme, Layout or Organisation information. Every save is written to the audit log.
ThemeThe organisation theme: brand, text on brand, background, surface, text, muted, lines, and corner radius. Tokens, not a stylesheet. A contrast check runs before publish. Failing contrast needs a confirmation. Everyone sees it on the next screen load, including the app bar, sign-in and the installed app colour.
LayoutThe published navigation: what sits on the bar and the rail, and what stays in More. The phone bar keeps the first four sidebar slots, then More. The rail holds eight. Home stays on the sidebar. Also the starting list layout on wide screens, cards or table. Phones stay on cards. Each person can still switch; that choice stays in the address.
Organisation informationThe name on the app bar, the legal name printed on receipts, contact, registered address, time zone, registrations such as 12A and 80G, and the logo. The organisation code and the sign-in link are read-only. Language, storage, the API limit and the financial year stay on the same screen under Other settings.
Same values as the BuilderTheme and layout are the published theme and navigation. Builder → Theme and Builder → Navigation edit the same values. This screen is the one an administrator uses. A module may ship a starting theme and a starting navigation; the administrator's publish replaces them for this organisation.
LogoPNG, JPEG or WebP, at most 1 MB, stored with the organisation. Shown on the app bar, the sign-in page and new receipts. A remote address is not loaded. Removing it returns the app bar to the name alone.
What this does not changeHome widgets, record forms, roles and permissions stay in the Builder. List filters, chips and a person's own cards-or-table choice stay as they are. The product name NGOOne stays on the app bar.
