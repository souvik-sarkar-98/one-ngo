Members · Membership requests

From application to member

A membership manager reviews each request, verifies it, and a second manager approves. Only after approval is the person’s login added, and then the request is completed, which creates the member, the joining fee, and the donor.

Nobody gets an account until a second manager has said yes.

01

The flow

Seven stops from application to member. A request can leave the flow at the review stop by being dismissed.

1 · Application arrives ExistingThe applicant submits the public form, or staff enter it. The request is numbered and its status is Submitted. The applicant gets a receipt email, and membership managers get an in-app notice and an email.
2 · Review ExistingA membership manager edits any details that need fixing. Then they choose Mark as verified or Dismiss.
3 · Mark as verified NewMark as verified sends the request to a second membership manager. The status stays Submitted until that manager decides.
4 · Approval ExistingThe second manager decides in Inbox › Approvals. Approve moves the request to Approved and emails the applicant. Reject leaves it Submitted for another look.
5 · Add the login NewOn an approved request, staff add the person under People, with name and email already filled in, then choose them on the request.
6 · Onboard ExistingClicking Onboard asks for the person, who must be linked to go on. Creates the member profile, the Rs 100 joining fee unless waived, and the donor profile unless an existing donor is linked.
7 · Completed ExistingFinal. The request keeps its record of who decided what.
  • Dismiss leaves the flow from Submitted. It needs a reason, which is emailed to the applicant. No member, donor, or login is created.
