Builder — Templates: message templates and report templates

One section, Templates, with two rows. A message template is what a person receives: in the app, by email, by SMS or on WhatsApp. A report template is the report kind: its parameters, data, calculations and one file, PDF or Excel. The PDF's page design is the report layout, kept inside the report. It is not a message template and is not listed under Message templates.

One section, two rowsTemplates holds Message templates and Report templates. Report groups stay in the same section as a kind, not a third row. No Email, Notification, Receipt or Document template kind is added.
The layout belongs to the reportThe report layout is edited on the report's Output tab, saved and published with the report, and versioned with it. It has no key of its own and cannot be picked by anything else.
One file per reportOutput is PDF or Excel, chosen once. PDF shows the report layout and page settings. Excel shows the sheets. The other format's settings are not shown.
Notify uses messages onlyAutomations and actions pick from Message templates. Reports never appear there. Publishing a report does not need, create or change a message template.

Here they become screens 1, 4 and 5. Receipt rules and receipt printing are not changed; receipt layouts are a separate plan.
