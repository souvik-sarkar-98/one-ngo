How money flows through OneNGO

Money comes in through three routes, sits in bank and cash ledger accounts, and leaves as approved expenses. Each real account is a money account attached to one of those ledger accounts. Every movement is one balanced double-entry journal, posted in the same save as the record that caused it.

2. How this meets the real bank

OneNGO does not connect to the bank. It never reads the bank's balance or statement and never moves money itself. The real bank only touches the books through a confirmation step, shown in the middle column. Each row ends in the same journal as section 1. The money account that receives UPI payments ('upi') is attached to BANK, so its balance is the books' BANK figure, not the bank's.

What exists in the code

  • UPI link built for the NGO's own UPI ID, with a reference the donor's bank carries in the narration.
  • Money accounts opened on the Money page or through the API (POST /api/money/accounts). With a ledgerCode (API only, not on the page), one attaches to an existing asset account such as BANK, and its balance is that account's balance. A code that is missing, closed, not an asset, or already attached is refused. Transfers between accounts post journal entries.
  • Statement matching (matchStatement): needs the reference in the line text and the same amount to the paisa. A line whose receipt would be for another amount stays unmatched.
  • Manual confirmation by someone holding payments:confirm, with the paid date and bank reference.
  • Gateway webhooks for Razorpay and Cashfree, tested only against a fake HTTP client. Verify against the provider sandboxes before going live.

What is not there

  • No bank API, bank feed or Account Aggregator link, and no read of the real balance.
  • No screen or file upload for statements. Matching is a server call that takes lines as data.
  • No gateway settlement or payout tracking, and no fee posting.
  • No outgoing payments. Expenses are recorded after the treasurer pays elsewhere.
  • Reconciliation exists in the API only (import, match, sign-off). There is no screen.

3. One event, one transaction

  1. A person moves a recordMark a donation Paid, a fee Paid, an earning Received, or a claim Paid. The required fields (date, account, method) must be filled and the date cannot be in the future.
  2. Posting rule firesThe rule for that record type and status builds a journal: debit one account, credit another, for the record's amount, dated on the paid / received date.
  3. Receipt is numberedDonations and membership fees only. A gap-free number is taken from the series, and the receipt keeps the organisation's details as they were that day.
  4. Everything commits togetherIf the journal is refused (closed period, unbalanced), the status change, the journal and the receipt all roll back. Books and records cannot drift apart.

6. What protects the money

Rules the database enforces

  • Every journal balances to the paisa (checked at commit).
  • Posted journals and their lines cannot be changed. A correction is a reversal plus a new entry.
  • A closed financial year refuses new postings. Reopening needs a reason and is audited.
  • Receipt numbers are gap-free. A void keeps its number and a replacement links back to it.
  • A record with ledger entries or receipts cannot be deleted.

Rules the workflows enforce

  • A payment date cannot be in the future.
  • A claim needs its bill to be submitted and a reason to be rejected.
  • Only paid, received or settled states post. Raised, expected, draft, submitted and approved never move money.
  • Permissions are per record type and per transition, for example the Finance and Expense approver roles.

7. What is not in the flow today

  • Processing fees are not posted. A donation's fee and net amount are shown on the receipt and the record, but the journal uses the full amount.
  • Physical assets do not post. The asset register records cost and disposal but makes no journal and has no depreciation.
  • Bank reconciliation has no screen. The API can import statement lines, match them to the books, book or ignore the rest, report the difference and sign off a month (permission money:reconcile). There is no CSV upload, no screen, and sign-off does not stop a back-dated ledger entry.
  • No funds or restricted money. Funds, statutory year-end features and the 80G register are planned, not built.
  • An approver can approve their own claim (accepted for 1.0.0).
  • The upi account needs its UPI ID. Installing Donations now creates the money account upi on BANK; an organisation that installed Donations earlier does not get it. Someone with money:accounts:manage sets the UPI ID with Edit on the Money page before a UPI link can be built.
  • One bank ledger account. One money account can attach to BANK, because each ledger account takes only one. A second real bank has no ledger account of its own until a module adds one. The bank-accounts module (BANK_2, BANK_3) was removed.
  • No bank balances report. It came with the removed module. The Money page still shows each money account's balance.
  • Modules ship by configuration. Only donations is on by default. Expenses, earnings and members go live when named in SHIPPED_MODULES.
