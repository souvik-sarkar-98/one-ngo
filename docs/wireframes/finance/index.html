How a person moves through them

  1. The treasurer opens Money page, sees balances for every account, and opens a second bank.
  2. A staff member records a donation or an expense in Record forms, choosing the real account. A claim goes to a second approver.
  3. Money moves between accounts with Transfer. An overdraft needs a reason that is logged.
  4. Each month the treasurer opens Account statement to check an account, then Bank reconciliation to compare it with the bank's CSV and sign off. At month or year end the Trial balance shows that the books as a whole are in order.

Open points the wireframes depend on

  • Overdraft check at two moments. The requester must be asked for the reason when sending a claim, but the books are written only when the second person approves. The server therefore needs a balance check at request time as well as at apply time.
  • Column mapping storage. Saved in the browser per account in this version. Moving it to the server would make it follow the person across devices.
  • Translations. New text is English first. The other five languages show it as untranslated until translated.
  • Sign-off does not lock the ledger. It freezes statement lines but a back-dated entry can still be posted into a signed-off month. The Sign off button should say so.
