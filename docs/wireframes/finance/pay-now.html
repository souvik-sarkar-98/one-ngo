Finance wireframes/Pay now

Pay now: choose the account WS6

The Pay now button on a donation creates a UPI payment link. Today it always targets the money account with key upi. The change lets the person choose which account receives the money, from the accounts that have a UPI ID, and records that account on the donation when payment is confirmed. All figures are made up.

What changes

  1. Received in is filled by the payment (new). On confirmation the payment's account is written to the donation's Received in field, through a new account entry in the payment's onPaid.fields. The server must remember which account each payment request was for; it does not store that today, so this needs a new column on the payment request. WS6
  2. Receive into (new). A choice list of money accounts that have a UPI ID, as a form field on the Pay now action. The action's account setting becomes an input instead of a fixed key. The list uses the account's key as its value, because payments look accounts up by key. WS6

Behaviour

  • With only one UPI account, the list is pre-selected and the dialog looks as it does today.
  • States C and D are unchanged except that they show the chosen account's UPI ID.
  • Confirming a payment never changes which account the link paid into. Amount mismatches are still held for review, as today.
  • The stored key upi is not renamed. Screens show the account's name, not its key.
